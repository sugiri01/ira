🎯 Kindergarten Interview Prep Guide for Ira

🌟 Get Ready for Kindergarten Success! 🌟

A comprehensive guide to help Ira shine in her kindergarten interview and prepare for school!

What to Expect in a Kindergarten Interview

Social Readiness

Interact appropriately with adults and follow directions

Academic Readiness

Basic foundational skills and knowledge

Communication

Express herself clearly and confidently

Independence

Handle basic self-care tasks

Academic Skills to Practice

Letters & Sounds

  • Recognize and name all uppercase letters A-Z
  • Recognize most lowercase letters a-z
  • Know the sounds that letters make
  • Spell and write her first name

Numbers & Counting

  • Count from 1 to 20 (some schools may ask up to 30)
  • Recognize written numbers 1-10
  • Count objects (like counting 5 crayons)
  • Understand 'more' and 'less'

Basic Concepts

  • Colors: red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink, black, white, brown
  • Shapes: circle, square, triangle, rectangle
  • Opposites: big/small, hot/cold, up/down, in/out
Communication & Social Skills
📋 Personal Information

Practice having Ira clearly state:

  • • Full name (first and last)
  • • Age and birthday
  • • Parents'/guardians' names
  • • Address (at least street name and city)
  • • Phone number (if age-appropriate)
👂 Following Directions

Practice simple 2-3 step directions:

  • • "Please pick up the red crayon, draw a circle, then put the crayon back"
  • • "Stand up, clap your hands three times, then sit down"
🤝 Social Skills
  • • Making eye contact when speaking
  • • Using "please," "thank you," and "excuse me"
  • • Raising hand to speak
  • • Taking turns
  • • Listening when others are talking
Sample Interview Questions
About Herself
  • ❓ What's your name?
  • ❓ How old are you?
  • ❓ When is your birthday?
  • ❓ What do you like to do for fun?
  • ❓ Do you have any pets?
Academic Questions
  • ❓ Can you tell me what letter this is?
  • ❓ What sound does this letter make?
  • ❓ Can you count these blocks?
  • ❓ What color is this?
  • ❓ What shape is this?
School-Related Questions
  • ❓ Are you excited about coming to school?
  • ❓ What do you think you'll do at school?
  • ❓ Who helps you at home?
  • ❓ What's your favorite book?
2-Week Practice Schedule
Foundation Building - Week 1
Days 1-3: Focus on personal information and basic letters
Days 4-5: Work on numbers and counting
Weekend: Review shapes, colors, and play educational games
Integration & Confidence - Week 2
Days 1-3: Practice following directions and fine motor skills
Days 4-5: Mock interview practice with family
Weekend: Light review and confidence building
Interview Day Tips
👕 What to Wear
  • • Comfortable, weather-appropriate clothing
  • • Shoes she can manage independently
  • • Avoid new clothes that might be uncomfortable
🎒 What to Bring
  • • Any required documents
  • • A comfort item if allowed
  • • Positive attitude and smile!
⏰ Before the Interview
  • • Get a good night's sleep
  • • Eat a healthy breakfast
  • • Arrive a few minutes early
  • • Use the bathroom before starting

🎉 Remember: The Goal is School Readiness

The interview isn't about perfection - it's about understanding where Ira is in her development and ensuring Kindergarten is the right fit.

✅ Building confidence - Celebrate what she knows! ✅ Making it fun - Learning should be enjoyable ✅ Staying positive - Your encouragement means everything ✅ Being patient - Every child develops at their own pace

Good luck, Ira! You're going to do great! 🌟
